# Returns & Refunds — FAQ

## How do I return an item?
Start a return from your order page and select the reason that matches your situation. We'll email a prepaid shipping label within 5 minutes. Drop the package at any courier point within 14 days of the return being started.

## How long do returns take?
Once we receive your returned item, it takes 3–5 business days to process. You'll get an email confirmation as soon as the refund is issued.

## When will I get my refund?
Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 3–5 business days of us receiving the returned item, and typically appear on your statement within 10 business days.

## What items cannot be returned?
Final-sale items, gift cards, and personal-care products (for hygiene reasons) cannot be returned. Everything else is eligible within 30 days of delivery, provided it's unused and in its original packaging.

## Do I pay return shipping?
No — for defective, damaged, or wrong items, return shipping is free. For "changed my mind" returns, a $4.99 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund.

## What if my item arrived damaged?
We're sorry about that. Damaged items are refunded in full, including original shipping. If the item is under $50, you don't need to send it back — just upload a photo of the damage. For items over $50, we'll email a prepaid return label.
